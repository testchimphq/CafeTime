## Prerequisites
- User must be logged in and navigated to the Dashboard.
- The user must have at least one other member in their contact list to send a message.

## Test Steps
- Step 1: Go to the Dashboard page.
- Step 2: Locate the messaging feature on the Dashboard, typically found in the sidebar or a designated section for messaging.
- Step 3: Click on the messaging section to open the messaging interface.
- Step 4: In the "To" field, enter the recipient's name or email address (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
- Step 5: In the message input box, type a test message (e.g., "Hello, this is a test message.").
- Step 6: Click on the "Send" button to send the message.
- Step 7: Verify that the message appears in the conversation history.

## Expected Behaviour
- The messaging interface should open successfully when accessed from the dashboard.
- The recipient's name or email address should be accepted without error.
- Upon clicking 'Send', the message should be sent without errors or delays.
- The sent message should appear in the conversation history, confirming successful transmission of the message.
